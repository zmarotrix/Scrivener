> [!WARNING]
> **Scrivener is in beta.** Expect bugs and changes between versions. If chat misbehaves, `/scrivener off` gives you the game's
> own chat back.

**Scrivener is chat, rebuilt for WoW Forever.** Windows you arrange in seconds, short lines you read at a glance, alerts
for the lines that matter to you, and chat that keeps working in dungeon fights.

## What it does

### Windows and tabs
Pick a layout the first time you log in, from pictures: one window, both sides, social, group or trader. **+** on any
tab bar adds a tab in one click (Guild, Group, Whispers, Trade & LFG, Loot & money, System, For you, Add-ons, or your
own mix of chats). Drag a tab onto a window's lines to show it beside them (or underneath), onto another tab bar to move
it, or onto empty screen for a new window. Arrange windows outlines them all; moving a window snaps it to the screen's
edges and to the other windows.

### Short lines
`12:41  Elowen Brightwater: Anyone up for BRD?` The chat is a coloured bar at the start of the line. Tabs that mix chats add
the chat's name, or a short one you choose: LookingForGroup as `LFG`, Global as `Glo`. Each short name is also a
command: `/glo hello`. Names are in class colour, and can be shown in full, as `Elowen B.` or as the first name only.
The same line from the same person within five minutes becomes one line with a count, and people coming online share
a line. Loot, money, rolls, experience and reputation read in one short shape with the game's own icons. Strangers
crafting and gathering near you stay out (a city is full of them); yours and your group's show.

### Conversations
The Whispers tab lists everyone you've whispered with, newest first, with their level and guild, who's online and
what's unread. Pick a person
for the conversation: their lines start with a green >, yours with a red <, and "New since you looked" marks where
you'd got to. Picking someone doesn't open the chat box; press Enter and it opens on a whisper to them. Right-click a
person for the game's menu, the same one their name has in chat.

### Trade & LFG as a board
A pane with channels switches from the stream of lines to a board: one card per person, their latest advert, how often
they posted it, the dungeon it's for, the roles it asks for, and the item's auction price when Fortune is installed.

### For you
Lines that say your name, and lines that match your alerts, gather in one tab, each with the reason. Alerts are words
(`brd heal*`, `-wtb` to rule lines out) or items you Shift-click in, for public channels or all chat, always or only
in cities, outside instances or when you're not in a group, and each tells you its own way: a toast, a sound, the
taskbar.

### Dungeon fights
WoW Forever hides what chat lines say from add-ons during dungeon fights. Scrivener still shows every line as it arrives,
like any other line. When the fight ends it reads them again, so names get their class colours, whispers join their
conversations, your name and alerts are checked, and the lines are kept in history.

### History and search
Whispers, guild, officer, group chat and For you survive reloads and logouts (30 days by default); channels, Say, loot
and system lines for two days. Your characters share one history, each showing
what was said on the others, unless you turn that off in Settings; then each shows only its own. Every character keeps its
own either way, so switching loses nothing. Search looks through all of it as you type, and Jump shows a result where it was said.

### The chat box
The game's own chat box, moved into Scrivener's window and restyled. Scrivener never rewrites it, so `/cast`, `/target` and
macros typed into it keep working in combat. A whisper to a first name fails on Forever; Scrivener offers the full name
and a one-click **Send again**. The pill at its right end is the game's chat menu: emotes, voice emotes and, on a
character with more than one, your languages.

### Everything the game's chat window did
The game's combat log, filters and all, as a Combat log tab. Battle.net whispers as conversations. Chat colours: click
a colour in Channel names. Joining and leaving channels: the game's own channel list, from the window menu. The game's
chat keys (Chat Page Up, Page Down, Bottom) scroll Scrivener, the time follows the game's 12-hour or seconds option, and
lines the game prints itself (the guild's message of the day, level-ups, /played) go to their own chat.

## Commands

`/scrivener` opens the settings. `/scrivener layout`, `/scrivener new`, `/scrivener arrange`, `/scrivener alert <words or item>`,
`/scrivener alerts`, `/scrivener names`, `/scrivener find <words>`, `/scrivener check`, `/scrivener off`, `/scrivener on`.

## Needs

Norgannon Core 1.35 or newer.
